An open letter to Pharma Quality all over the world

QUALITY IS NOT AN ACT; IT IS A HABIT

ARISTOTLE’S QUOTE: OVERLY QUOTED CLICHÉ OR A LIVING REALITY?

19 August 2026

Dear Quality Professionals

Thank you all for your perseverance to follow a task through, attention to detail, fearlessness to call out a problem – which many of us would have overlooked. You have invisibly kept people safe, healthy with such effectiveness – that those of us, our children, our parents who have taken medication, take your work for granted.

In 2026, August – the stars have finally aligned, it is your time!

We are blessed to have an expert team of software professionals who have an in-depth, working knowledge of Pharma Quality and Compliance. This team has created a state-of-the-art Electronic Quality Management System: Inglasia Intuitor™ v1.0.

The system works FOR Quality, Quality does not work for the system

With a background in finance, it has taken me 15 years to begin to grasp the full extent of what Quality do – the level of precision and perseverance required to see a process through.

Taking the example of a Change Control process. Inglasia Intuitor™ took me through the entire workflow. I had previously believed that a Change Control consisted of just raising the change control; documenting the areas of change; and final sign off. The workflow took me through the training requirements – just to permit me to raise a change control; the rigour of the Impact assessment; Risk Assessment; the levels of review and approval – in a practical and meaningful way. Hours’ worth of training courses would never have made such an indelible mark.

Every question I had was pre-empted by information icons with regulatory prompts (I had specifically requested these) to teach the user when they were unsure.

The final paradox, I had a childhood phobia of computers! From someone who avoided using a computer like the plague or virus (pardon the pun), to working with a team to design a software that sits within the Pharmaceutical profession. My main requirement – an eQMS must be easy and inviting to use. Inglasia Intuitor™ invites the user on a journey and tutors them in Quality in the most intuitive manner, whilst making the learning journey feel like a business class flight: the system understands your needs, even before you do.

Bringing Quality into the forefront – new age – new technology. We have not taken the investor-driven route to market. Our goal is to serve our industry. Let us help each other to provide a sustainable and accessible service.

From years of listening to my colleagues’ difficulties on a particular client assignment – the team has created what I believe to be a CAPA so thorough and so effective, that Quality will never go back to being misunderstood or ignored. Quality will take its central place within Pharma.

Thank you all once again for the wonderful work you do.

Here’s to raising a new Change Control for Inglasia Intuitor™ v1.0!

Can Quality ever be a Habit and not just an Act? Absolutely, yes!!

Best wishes

Anju Nadarajah
Director and Founder
