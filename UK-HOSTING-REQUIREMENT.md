# Production hosting requirement — United Kingdom

The Inglasia Intuitor production site, including `quality-system-quiz.html`, must be deployed on infrastructure physically located in the **United Kingdom** or in the hosting provider’s explicit **UK** region.

This requirement cannot be enforced from the site’s HTML, CSS, or JavaScript. It must be selected in the hosting provider’s deployment, storage, CDN, and any serverless-function configuration before publication.

Before publishing, confirm that the provider’s deployment target is described as **United Kingdom**, **UK**, or a clearly UK-located region such as **London**. If a content delivery network is used, configure it so the origin remains in the required UK region and confirm its data-residency terms meet the project’s requirements.
