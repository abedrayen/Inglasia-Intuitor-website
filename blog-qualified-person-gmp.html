What is the most time-consuming aspect of a Qualified Person's role?

A conversation on QP batch release & GMP

Anju Nadarajah, Managing Director, in conversation with Qualified Person Lynne Butcher

27 August 2026

This video is a short discussion, between the Managing Director of Inglasia Ltd (Anju Nadarajah) and The Qualified Person (Lynne Butcher) on the aspects of the QP's role in ensuring the manufacturing of medicinal product, is in accordance with the product's regulatory approved specifications and GMP, every time.

Anju Nadarajah & Lynne Butcher, on the QP's role in batch release

All aspects of manufacturing, of every batch, needs to be scrutinised and this can take a considerable amount of time, if documentation is all in paper format or managed through multiple electronic systems.

Lynne Butcher discusses how simplifying processes, through integrated and automated electronic Quality Management Systems, can support in optimising the batch release process.
